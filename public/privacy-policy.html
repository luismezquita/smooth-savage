Privacy Policy

Smooth Savage · Effective date: May 2026

Smooth Savage is a mobile app designed to help you discover fresh foods, superfoods, and smoothie recipes. We built it with privacy in mind — the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

No personal data collected

Smooth Savage does not collect any personal data. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other identifying information. There is no account registration or login required to use the app.

No tracking or analytics

We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, or any third-party tracking services. Your usage of the app is not monitored or recorded in any way.

Favorites stored locally only

When you save items to your Favorites, that data is stored exclusively on your device. It is never uploaded to a server or shared with anyone. If you uninstall the app, your saved favorites will be removed along with it.

Offline-first app

Smooth Savage is designed to work fully offline. All content — foods, superfoods, smoothie recipes, and images — is bundled with the app. No internet connection is required after installation, and no data is sent to external servers during use.

No third-party sharing

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to share with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or transfer any user information to anyone.

Children's privacy

Smooth Savage does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is safe for all ages.
